# Code of conduct

DevDeck follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming, respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests and discussions. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not accepted.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour concerns you, contact the maintainer, [@abhishek-3289](https://github.com/abhishek-3289), privately on GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially, and the maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads or block people who break this code.
